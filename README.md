# ⚔️ Norse Node
### Find Your Space. Connect Your Peers. Elevate Your Learning.

**Norse Node** is cretaed for the main purpose of a student-focused platform designed to improve campus productivity, academic collaboration, and student connectivity.

The platform aims to solve two common challenges students face: finding suitable study spaces and connecting with classmates who share similar academic interests.

## 🎯 Project Overview

Students often waste valuable time searching for convenient places to study or struggling to find classmates who can provide academic assistance.

Norse Node addresses these challenges by providing a centralized platform where students can discover study locations and connect with peers through course-specific and major-based communities.

Our goal is to create a more connected, collaborative, and productive campus experience.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📍 Study Space Discovery
- Help students discover convenient study locations on campus.
- Reduce time spent searching for suitable study environments.
- Improve overall student productivity and time management.

### 💬 Course-Based Communities
- Create and join dedicated servers for specific courses.
- Connect with classmates taking similar classes.
- Ask questions, exchange knowledge, and collaborate on coursework.

### 🎓 Major-Based Networking
- Connect with students pursuing the same academic major.
- Build academic communities around shared interests.
- Encourage mentorship, networking, and peer-to-peer learning.

### 🤝 Academic Collaboration
- Share educational resources and study materials.
- Receive academic assistance from fellow students.
- Foster a supportive and collaborative learning environment.

## 💡 The Problem We're Solving

College students frequently experience difficulties locating appropriate study environments and connecting with the right people for academic support.

Norse Node brings these essential resources together into one accessible platform, allowing students to spend less time searching and more time learning.

## 🌟 Project Vision

Our vision is to build a connected campus ecosystem where students can easily find study spaces, form academic communities, and support one another throughout their educational journey.

By combining study space discovery with student networking, Norse Node aims to make campus life more efficient, accessible, and collaborative.

### 🖥️ Norse Node — Welcome & Login Interface

The **Norse Node Welcome Page** serves as the entry point to the platform, featuring a modern, student-friendly interface inspired by Northern Kentucky University's signature black and gold colors.

The interface incorporates a campus-themed background, a live digital clock displaying the local time in Highland Heights, Kentucky, and a streamlined login form where students can enter their first name, last name, and preferred alias.

**Key Interface Highlights:**
- **University-Inspired Design:** A sleek black-and-gold aesthetic reflecting NKU's identity.
- **Live Local Clock:** A digital clock displaying the current time in Highland Heights, KY.
- **Simplified Student Login:** An intuitive form collecting basic user information and a preferred alias.
- **Responsive Visual Layout:** A clean, centered design emphasizing accessibility and ease of navigation.
- **Campus Integration:** A university-themed background that creates a familiar and welcoming experience.

**Purpose:** To provide students with an intuitive starting point for accessing Norse Node's study space discovery and peer collaboration features.
